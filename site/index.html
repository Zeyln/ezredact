What your phone might still keep

EzRedact can't control your phone or browser. They may keep:

  • App switcher previews and tab thumbnails. EzRedact covers the screen when it goes to the background, but on iPhone the preview can be taken first.
  • A temporary copy of a photo you pick, made by the system photo picker.
  • Clipboard history and synced clipboards (if you use Copy).
  • On some Android phones, memory moved to storage when RAM runs low.

To be safe, close EzRedact from the app switcher when you're done.
